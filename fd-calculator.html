How is FD maturity calculated?

Maturity = P × (1 + r ÷ n)n × t, where P is the deposit, r the annual rate, n the compounding frequency per year and t the tenure in years. Most banks compound quarterly.

Frequently asked questions

Is FD interest taxable?

Yes, FD interest is added to your income and taxed at your slab rate. Banks deduct TDS above certain limits.

Does a senior-citizen rate apply?

Enter the rate your bank offers you — senior citizens typically get an extra 0.25–0.75%.
