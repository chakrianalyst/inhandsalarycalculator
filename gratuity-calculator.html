Gratuity formula

For employees covered under the Payment of Gratuity Act: Gratuity = (Last drawn basic + DA) × 15 ÷ 26 × years of service. Service of more than 6 months in the last year is rounded up to a full year. For employees not covered, the divisor is 30.

Frequently asked questions

When do I receive gratuity?

On resignation, retirement, death or disability — provided you completed 5 years (waived for death or disability).

Is gratuity taxable?

Up to ₹20 lakh is exempt for employees covered under the Gratuity Act. Amounts above are taxable as salary.
