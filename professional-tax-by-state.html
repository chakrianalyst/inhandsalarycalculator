Professional tax by state

Professional tax is a small state-level tax that your employer deducts from your salary. It is capped at ₹2,500 a year by law, and it is deducted from your pay before it reaches your bank account. Rules and slabs differ by state, and some states charge nothing.

Points to know

  • The deduction is spread across the year, with the last month often adjusted so the yearly total adds up.
  • Professional tax is deductible from your taxable salary in the old regime.
  • Slabs change from time to time, and different employers may follow slightly different practice. Always check your payslip.

Frequently asked questions

What is the maximum professional tax?

The law caps it at ₹2,500 a year per person.

Which states have no professional tax?

Several states and union territories, including Delhi, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan, do not levy it. Check your own state.

Is professional tax part of income tax?

No. It is a separate state tax, though it can be deducted from taxable salary in the old regime.
