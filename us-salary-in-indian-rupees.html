Why the same salary saves so much more in one city

New York City adds state and city income tax on top of federal tax, and its rent is about twice that of Austin or Dallas. Both come out of the same pay, so the savings gap is much wider than the salary gap. That is why an offer is best compared on savings, not on the converted salary.

What this leaves out

These are first-year figures in today’s money, for a single person with no 401(k) contributions. Over several years, raises, investment returns, the rupee’s fall against the dollar and trips home all change the picture, and a partner’s income can change it completely.
