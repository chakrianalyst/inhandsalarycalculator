Frequently asked questions

What is in-hand salary?

In-hand (take-home) salary is what you actually receive in your bank account each month after employee PF, professional tax and income tax (TDS) are deducted from your gross pay. It is usually 70–85% of monthly CTC.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. Every calculation runs locally in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded or saved on a server.

Which tax year do the calculators use?

FY 2026-27 (AY 2027-28) slabs, including the ₹75,000 standard deduction and the ₹12 lakh rebate limit under the new regime.

Will the result match my payslip exactly?

It will be very close, but employers structure pay differently (DA, reimbursements, insurance, etc.), so use it as a reliable estimate and confirm with your HR or payroll team.
