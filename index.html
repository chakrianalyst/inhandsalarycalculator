Frequently asked questions

What is in-hand salary?

In-hand (take-home) salary is what you actually receive in your bank account each month after employee PF, professional tax and income tax (TDS) are deducted from your gross pay. It is often well below CTC ÷ 12, because CTC includes items such as employer PF and gratuity that are not paid out monthly.

Is my data stored anywhere?

Every calculation runs in your browser, and what you type is not sent to our servers. Some tools keep your entries in your own browser’s storage so the page remembers them, and you can clear that any time. If you use the Share button, the link contains your inputs in its address, so share it only with people you trust.

Which tax year do the calculators use?

Tax Year 2026-27 (FY 2026-27) slabs, including the ₹75,000 standard deduction and the ₹12 lakh rebate limit under the new regime.

Will the result match my payslip exactly?

It will be very close, but employers structure pay differently (DA, reimbursements, insurance, etc.), so use it as a reliable estimate and confirm with your HR or payroll team.
