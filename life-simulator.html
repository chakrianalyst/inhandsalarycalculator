Plan your whole life in money, not just next month

Big life decisions rarely happen one at a time. A home loan arrives just as a child is born; a career jump funds the car; a career break stretches your savings. The Life Money Simulator puts these events on a single timeline so you can see their combined effect on your net worth and liquid savings.

How to use it

  1. Enter your age, income, living costs and current savings.
  2. Switch on the life events that may apply and drag each one to the age it happens.
  3. Watch the chart. Icons mark each event; hover (or tap) the chart for the numbers at any age.
  4. Flip to Today's money to remove the effect of inflation and judge results in familiar rupees.

What the green and blue lines mean

The blue line is your total net worth: investments plus home value minus any loan. The green line is just your liquid investments. A large gap means much of your wealth is locked in a house — which is fine, as long as the green line stays above zero when you stop working.

Frequently asked questions

What is 'financial freedom age'?

The first age at which your investments, withdrawn at 3.5% a year, could cover that year's spending without touching the principal.

Are taxes included?

Yes. Income tax is computed each year on the new-regime slabs, with slabs assumed to grow with inflation so later years are not distorted.

What happens if I run out of cash?

The simulator assumes you borrow at 12% and flags the year your liquid savings go negative as a 'cash crunch'.
