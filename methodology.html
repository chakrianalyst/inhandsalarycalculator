How we calculate

Transparency builds trust. This page lists the rules behind the salary and tax numbers, plus what the calculators deliberately simplify.

Income tax: FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27), salaried individuals

New regime (default)

  • Slabs: nil up to ₹4L; 5% to ₹8L; 10% to ₹12L; 15% to ₹16L; 20% to ₹20L; 25% to ₹24L; 30% above.
  • Standard deduction: ₹75,000. Employer NPS (80CCD(2)) up to 14% of basic.
  • Section 87A rebate up to ₹60,000, so taxable income up to ₹12L is tax-free, with marginal relief just above it.

Old regime

  • Slabs: nil up to ₹2.5L; 5% to ₹5L; 20% to ₹10L; 30% above.
  • Standard deduction ₹50,000; HRA exemption; 80C up to ₹1.5L; 80CCD(1B) up to ₹50,000; 80D; home-loan interest up to ₹2L; employer NPS up to 10% of basic; professional tax.
  • Section 87A rebate up to ₹12,500 for taxable income up to ₹5L.

Both regimes

  • 4% health & education cess on tax plus surcharge.
  • Surcharge of 10% above ₹50L, 15% above ₹1Cr, 25% above ₹2Cr (and 37% above ₹5Cr in the old regime; capped at 25% in the new regime), each with marginal relief.

Salary structure assumptions

  • Basic salary defaults to 40% of CTC; HRA to 50% of basic; both are editable.
  • Employer and employee PF are 12% of basic (optionally capped at ₹15,000 of basic).
  • Gratuity is 4.81% of basic and sits inside CTC but is not paid monthly.
  • Professional tax defaults to ₹200 a month (actual amounts vary by state); it is deductible only in the old regime.
  • Variable pay is assumed to be paid in full and spread evenly over 12 months.

Other calculators

  • EMI: reducing-balance formula. SIP / FD: standard compound-growth formulas, before tax.
  • Gratuity: (last basic + DA) × 15 ÷ 26 × years, with the ₹20 lakh exemption limit.
  • HRA: least of actual HRA, rent minus 10% of basic, and 50%/40% of basic.
  • Net worth score, FIRE, rent-vs-buy and life simulator: educational models with their assumptions stated on each page. They are not predictions.

What is not included

Dearness allowance, LTA, reimbursements, arrears, capital-gains tax, TDS timing differences, state-specific rules, employee NPS, and leave-encashment effects are not modelled. Real payslips can differ.

Quality checks

The tax engine has automated tests for known values, rebate and relief boundaries, and a sweep across incomes up to ₹30 crore in both regimes. If tax law changes in a Budget, the rules live in a single file so they can be updated quickly.
