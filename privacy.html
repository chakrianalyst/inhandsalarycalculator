Privacy Policy & Disclaimer

Your calculations stay on your device

Calculators run entirely in your browser. The salary, loan, savings and other figures you type are not sent to our servers.

What is stored in your browser

  • Your theme choice and your cookie-notice choice.
  • The Net Worth calculator saves your entries, and any monthly snapshots you choose to save, in this browser's local storage so they are there next time. It also remembers the last gold rate you typed. Use “Clear all” and delete a snapshot to erase them.
  • When you tap a suggested next step under a result, the numbers it carries to the next calculator are kept in this browser tab for a moment and deleted as soon as that page opens.
  • If you share a link, it contains your inputs in its address. Anyone with that link can see those numbers, so share it only with people you trust. The share picture is made on your device and shows only your result, not what you typed. We never receive it.

Analytics and advertising

We count visits with Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It sets no cookies and does not identify you. It records the page you visited (without the numbers in the address), the website that sent you, your rough country, your device type and browser, and simple actions such as “used a calculator” or “shared a result”. It never receives the figures you type. If your browser sends a “Do Not Track” signal, nothing is counted.

If we enable Google Analytics or Google AdSense (ads that keep the site free), those services may set cookies, and we will ask first in a cookie notice. You can decline there, manage ad personalisation in your Google account, or block cookies in your browser.

Affiliate links

Links marked “Sponsored” are affiliate links. If you sign up through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This never affects calculator results.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13.
