Privacy Policy & Disclaimer

Your calculations stay on your device

Calculators run entirely in your browser. The salary, loan, savings and other figures you type are not sent to our servers.

What is stored in your browser

  • Your theme choice and your cookie-notice choice.
  • The Net Worth calculator saves your entries, and any monthly snapshots you choose to save, in this browser's local storage so they are there next time. It also remembers the last gold rate you typed. Use “Clear all” and delete a snapshot to erase them.
  • If you use “Share”, the link contains your inputs in its address. Anyone with that link can see those numbers, so share it only with people you trust.

Analytics and advertising

When enabled, we use Google Analytics (with IP anonymisation) to understand which tools are useful, and Google AdSense to show ads that keep the site free. These services may set cookies. You can decline in the cookie notice, manage ad personalisation in your Google account, or block cookies in your browser.

Affiliate links

Links marked “Sponsored” are affiliate links. If you sign up through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This never affects calculator results.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13.
