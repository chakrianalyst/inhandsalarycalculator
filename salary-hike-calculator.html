Why is my in-hand hike smaller than my CTC hike?

Part of every raise goes to higher income tax (a bigger slice falls in higher slabs) and to extra PF and gratuity. So a 15% CTC hike usually translates to a slightly lower percentage increase in take-home pay — especially if the raise pushes you across a tax slab.

Negotiating tip

Ask for the raise in cash components (basic and special allowance) rather than in benefits you won't fully use. Always compare offers on in-hand salary, not CTC alone.
