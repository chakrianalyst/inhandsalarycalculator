Variable pay in CTC: how much you actually get

Variable pay is counted in your CTC but not paid with your monthly salary, and often not in full. Here is what it means for your monthly in-hand, how much of it is left after tax, and how to compare offers with different variable pay.

Why variable pay is taxed so much

Your variable pay is added to the rest of your salary for the year, so it is taxed at the highest rate you reach. On ₹20 LPA, the tax on the ₹3 lakh variable is {{VP_20_TAX}}, about {{VP_20_TAXPCT}}.

How to compare offers with variable pay

  • Compare the fixed pay first: CTC minus variable pay.
  • Ask what percentage of variable pay was actually paid last year, and how often it is paid.
  • Plan your monthly budget, rent and EMIs on the fixed in-hand only. Use variable pay for savings or one-off spending.
  • Check whether your next hike is calculated on fixed pay or on the full CTC.

Frequently asked questions

Is variable pay included in CTC?

Usually, yes. Offer letters often show it as a separate line within the CTC, at its full target amount.

Is variable pay paid monthly?

Rarely. Most companies pay it every quarter, every six months or once a year, after results and ratings are known.

What if I leave before it is paid?

In many companies you get nothing for that period if you are not on the payroll on the payout date. Check your offer letter or HR policy.

How is tax on variable pay deducted?

Your employer adds it to your salary for the year and deducts the extra tax (TDS), often from the month it is paid, so that month’s salary can look lower.
