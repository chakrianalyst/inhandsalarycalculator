How is in-hand salary calculated?

Your CTC (Cost to Company) is the total an employer spends on you in a year. It includes items you never receive as cash — employer PF and gratuity — so your take-home is always lower than CTC ÷ 12.

  1. Gross salary = CTC − employer PF − gratuity − employer NPS (basic + HRA + special allowance + variable).
  2. Subtract employee PF (12% of basic) and professional tax.
  3. Subtract income tax (TDS) computed on taxable income after your deductions.
  4. What remains, divided by 12, is your monthly in-hand salary.

Which regime is better?

The new regime wins for most people with fewer deductions. The old regime can win if you claim large HRA, 80C, 80D and home-loan interest deductions. This calculator computes both and tells you the difference in rupees.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my in-hand salary much lower than CTC/12?

Because CTC includes employer PF and gratuity, plus your own PF, professional tax and income tax are deducted before payout.

Is PF always 12% of basic?

PF is 12% of basic wages. Some employers cap it at ₹15,000 of basic (₹1,800/month). Use the “Limit PF” switch to model that.

Is variable pay included?

Yes — the calculator assumes you receive variable pay in full and spreads it evenly over 12 months for the monthly figure.

Is professional tax deductible?

Yes under the old regime. It is not allowed as a deduction in the new regime, but it is still deducted from your pay.
