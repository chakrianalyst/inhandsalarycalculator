How to read your offer letter: the CTC breakup, line by line

Your offer letter says ₹18 LPA, but your first salary is far less than ₹1.5 lakh. Here is what each line of a typical CTC breakup means, which parts reach your bank every month, and what to ask HR before you sign.

The short version
  • Your CTC has three kinds of money: pay you get every month, pay you may get once a year (variable pay or bonus), and money you don’t get now (employer PF and gratuity).
  • On a ₹18 LPA offer with 10% variable pay, about {{OL_INHAND}} a month reaches your bank, against the {{OL_CTC_MO}} you might expect from CTC ÷ 12.
  • Before you sign, ask how much of the CTC is variable, how much of it is usually paid, and whether PF is 12% of basic or capped at ₹3,000 a month.

A typical breakup, explained

This is how a ₹18 LPA offer with 10% variable pay is often laid out: basic at 40% of CTC, HRA at half of basic, PF at 12% of basic and gratuity inside the CTC. The special allowance is whatever is left to make up the total.

{{OL_TABLE}}
  • Basic salary is the base for your PF and gratuity. A higher basic means more PF and gratuity but less special allowance.
  • HRA is fully taxed in the new tax regime. In the old regime, part of it is tax-free if you pay rent.
  • Special allowance is ordinary taxable salary. It balances the CTC.
  • Variable pay is a target, not a promise. It is usually paid once or twice a year, depending on your rating and the company’s results.
  • Employer PF goes into your PF account, not your bank. It is still your money, for later.
  • Gratuity is paid only when you leave, and usually only after 5 years of service. Many people who switch jobs earlier never receive it.

Questions to ask HR before you sign

  • How much of the CTC is variable, and how much was actually paid last year? See how variable pay changes your in-hand.
  • Is PF 12% of my basic, or capped at ₹3,000 a month? It changes your in-hand by thousands a month: see 12% PF or capped ₹3,000 PF.
  • Are gratuity and insurance included in the CTC? If they are, the CTC looks bigger but your salary does not change.
  • Is a joining bonus included in the CTC? If it is, your second year’s pay will be lower. See joining bonus: tax and clawback.
  • Which items are one-time? Relocation, sign-on bonuses and stock grants are not salary you get every year.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my in-hand so much less than CTC ÷ 12?

Because CTC includes money that is not paid monthly (variable pay, employer PF, gratuity), and your own PF, professional tax and income tax come out of your salary.

What is “gross salary” in an offer letter?

Usually your CTC minus employer PF, gratuity and other employer benefits. It is your salary before your own PF and tax, and it often includes variable pay.

Is a higher basic salary better?

It means more PF and gratuity, which is saving for later, but less money in hand each month. In the old regime it also allows a bigger tax-free HRA.

What does “fixed CTC” mean?

The CTC without variable pay and one-time items. It is the best number for comparing two offers.
