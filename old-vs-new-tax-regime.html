Old vs New Tax Regime which wins?

India's two income-tax regimes trade lower rates for fewer deductions. The right choice depends mostly on one number: how much you can genuinely claim in deductions.

Up to ₹12.75 lakh of salary the new regime charges no tax at all, so the old regime has to bring its tax down to zero as well to match it. That takes very large deductions, which is why ₹12.75 lakh needs more deductions than ₹15 lakh in the table. Above ₹25 lakh, you would need eight lakh or more in deductions, which is rare without a large home-loan interest claim.

Frequently asked questions

Which tax regime is better for me?

If you claim few deductions, the new regime usually wins, and income up to ₹12.75 lakh of salary is effectively tax-free. The old regime can win if your total deductions are large. Use the table above to see how large.

Can I switch between regimes every year?

Salaried employees can choose a regime each year when declaring to their employer and finally when filing the return. Rules differ for business income, so check with a professional.

Is HRA available in the new regime?

No. HRA exemption, 80C, 80D and home-loan interest on a self-occupied property are old-regime deductions.
