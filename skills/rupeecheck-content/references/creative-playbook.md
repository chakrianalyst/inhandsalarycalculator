# Creative playbook

Good finance content makes a reader think "I never knew that" or "that's not what I assumed". This file is how to find that moment from real numbers, then shape it.

Contents: [Explore first](#explore-first) · [Angle types](#angle-types) · [Hooks](#hooks) · [Choosing](#choosing-the-angle) · [Seasonal calendar](#seasonal-calendar) · [Topic to tool](#topic-to-tool)

## Explore first
Do not start from a headline. Run a **sweep** and look at the output.
- Same tool, five inputs: `salary` at ₹8, 12, 15, 20, 30 lakh; `sip` goal at 10, 15, 20, 25 years; `emi` with and without ₹5,000 extra a month.
- Same inputs, one lever moved: abroad rent −20%, the rupee falling 3% vs 5%, step-up 0% vs 10%.
- Look for: a ratio that flips (a tax-free band), a line that bends (tax kicks in), something much bigger or smaller than people assume, a comparison that is closer than expected.
Write down 3 to 5 candidate findings with their numbers before choosing.

## Angle types
| Angle | What it does | Example from the site's own numbers |
|---|---|---|
| **Myth vs math** | Names a common belief and breaks it with one figure | "₹12 LPA is not ₹1 lakh a month. It's ₹88,276." |
| **Where it goes** | Shows a total splitting into parts, with a twist | Of the ₹1.4 L gap, ₹1.38 L is your own PF and gratuity |
| **The small lever** | One input moved a little changes the result a lot | Every 20% less rent abroad adds about ₹1.7 Cr by year 10 |
| **The cost of waiting** | Compares starting now vs later | ₹1 crore: ₹44,636 a month in 10 years vs ₹10,871 in 20 |
| **Closer than you think** | A comparison people expect to be lopsided | Year-1 savings: ₹24.8 L in San Francisco vs ₹19.9 L in Bengaluru |
| **The hidden threshold** | A rule that creates a cliff or a free zone | Salary up to about ₹12.75 L pays no tax in the new regime |
| **Interest is the loan** | Reframes a cost people ignore | ₹54.1 L interest on a ₹50 L loan, 82% of the first EMI is interest |
| **What if…** | A scenario the reader can copy | ₹5,000 extra a month ends the loan 4.4 years sooner |
| **Mistake list** | 3 to 5 common errors, each with a figure | Comparing CTC instead of in-hand when choosing offers |
| **Answer-the-question** | Reddit and Quora: solve the asker's problem | Show their numbers worked through |

## Hooks
Patterns that work (adapt, do not copy):
- "X is not Y. It's Z." (a number replacing an assumption)
- "Everyone compares A. The number that decides it is B."
- "I ran the numbers on ___. The result surprised me."
- "How much does ___ cost over 20 years? More than the loan itself."
- A question the reader can answer in their head: "What's your in-hand on ₹15 LPA?"
Avoid: "Did you know", "Shocking", "Secrets", anything that promises riches, and fear tactics.

A strong hook states a **specific number** in the first line and makes the reader curious about the working.

## Choosing the angle
Score each candidate 1 to 5 on: **surprise** (would a reader say "huh"), **verifiable** (a tool run plus an independent check exists), **relevant** (maps to a RupeeCheck page), **shareable** (someone would send it to a colleague), **safe** (no advice, no fragile assumption doing the heavy lifting). Drop anything under 3 on safe or verifiable. Choose the highest total, and keep the runner-up to offer the user.

## Seasonal calendar
Match the topic to what people are worrying about now.
| When | What people think about | Good topics |
|---|---|---|
| January to March | Tax saving, Budget (1 February), investing before year end | Old vs new regime, 80C and NPS, HRA, tax-free salary band |
| April to June | New financial year, appraisals, hikes, switching jobs | Salary hike in-hand, offer comparison, CTC vs in-hand |
| July | ITR filing | Regime choice, HRA proofs, FD interest tax |
| August to September | Mid-year planning, festive purchases ahead | SIP goals, EMI planning, rent vs buy |
| October to November | Diwali, bonuses, job changes | Bonus tax, gold and net worth, offer comparisons |
| December | Year-end review | Net worth by age, FIRE, SIP step-up |
Budget day and the days after are the biggest chance: reply with verified numbers fast, but only after you have run the new rules in the engine (the engine must be updated first).
