# Compliance and guardrails

A finance brand lives on trust. These rules keep the user safe from legal trouble, platform bans and embarrassment.

## Education, not advice
- Show what the maths gives **under stated assumptions**. Do not tell a person what to do with their money, which fund or stock to buy, or which loan to take.
- In India, giving personalised investment advice without registration is regulated. Keep language general: "Here is what ₹10,000 a month does at 12%", not "You should invest ₹10,000 in X".
- Never promise or imply returns. Always say a return is an assumption.

## Standard lines (use the shortest that fits)
- Tax posts: "Estimate for salaried individuals, Tax Year 2026-27. Please confirm with a CA."
- SIP, FIRE, net worth: "Returns are not guaranteed. This is not investment advice."
- Abroad and return: "An estimate under the assumptions shown. Tax rules, rents and exchange rates change."
- Gratuity and labour law: "Based on the labour-code rules as understood at the time; confirm with your employer or a labour-law professional."
- Salary: "Your payslip is the final word."

## Disclosure
- Reddit and Quora: say you built the site ("I'm the maker"). It is the rule on most communities and it builds trust.
- If affiliate links are ever added, mark them (the site already labels them "Sponsored").
- Do not imply endorsement by any bank, company, regulator or person.

## No fake engagement or identities
- Never write a post or comment as if from a different, ordinary person.
- Never create or suggest extra accounts to boost a post, ask for upvotes, buy followers or likes, or reply to your own thread from another account.
- Never invent testimonials, case studies, "a friend of mine" stories or survey numbers.
- Real stories are fine only if the user provides them and they are true.

## Platform and community rules
- Read the community's rules before drafting a Reddit or Facebook-group post, and follow them even when they forbid links.
- Do not mass-post identical text. Tailor each reply to the question.
- Do not DM people uninvited.
- Respect each platform's rules on repeated promotional posting.

## Privacy
- Do not ask users to share their real salary or payslip in public posts. Invite them to try their own numbers privately in the tool.
- When quoting the site's privacy: calculations run in the browser, and what you type is not sent to the site's servers. Do not say "your data never leaves your browser".

## Sensitive topics
- Do not exploit fear (job loss, retirement panic) or shame people for their choices.
- Be careful with comparisons of countries and cities; stay with the numbers and the assumptions.

## When in doubt
Write the safer version, add the assumption, and tell the user what you were unsure about.
