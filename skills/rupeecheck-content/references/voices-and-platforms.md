# Voices and platforms

Each platform rewards a different kind of person speaking. Using the same text everywhere is the most common mistake. The voice below was chosen for what works on that platform and for what protects the user: the brand speaks where the brand is expected (Instagram, X, Facebook), and a **named, honest human** speaks where people distrust brands (Reddit, Quora).

## Instagram
**Voice: the RupeeCheck brand, as a clear and friendly explainer.** "We", rarely "I". Visual first: the image does the persuading, the caption supports it.

**Format that works: a carousel, 6 to 8 slides, 1080×1350.**
1. Hook slide: a myth or a surprising number (use the `myth` or `stat` template). The reader should want to swipe.
2. Slides 2 to 6: one idea and one number per slide (`stat`, `compare`, `bars`).
3. Last slide: the takeaway in one line, "Save this for appraisal season", and the page address.

**Caption:** 60 to 150 words. First line repeats the hook (only the first line shows before "more"). Then two or three lines of value, then a question. Add 3 to 5 hashtags at the end (#PersonalFinance #CTC #SalaryIndia). Links in captions are not clickable: write "Link in bio" and make sure the user's bio link is the site or the right page.

**Also available:** a 20 to 30 second reel script with on-screen text per beat. Write the script and the on-screen text; the user films or screen-records the calculator.

**Do not:** use hashtags in bulk, use stock-photo style language ("unlock your financial freedom"), post a slide that needs squinting to read (minimum body text is what the renderer produces), or cram two numbers per slide.

**Example caption (SIP for ₹1 crore):**
> ₹1 crore needs ₹44,636 a month if you start 10 years out. Start 20 years out and it's ₹10,871. 👀
> Time does most of the work. The money you put in drops from ₹53.6 L to ₹26.1 L.
> Swipe for the full table 👉 Which number surprised you most?
> Link in bio to try your own goal. (12% a year is an assumption, not a promise.)
> #SIP #PersonalFinance #MutualFunds

## Facebook
**Voice: warm and neighbourly, "you" and "your family". Relatable stories and household money, written the way someone would explain it over chai.** Facebook skews older and family-minded; people respond to payslips, home loans, children's education and parents' health costs.

**Format:** one image (`compare` or `stat`, 1080×1350 or 1200×630 for a link preview) plus 80 to 200 words of text. A short story or a "have you noticed this?" opening works better than a statistic opening. End with a question that invites people to share their own number or experience.

**Links:** posts with outside links tend to get less reach. Put the link in the **first comment** and say "link in the first comment". In Facebook Groups, follow the group's rules: many ban promotion. Offering the answer and the working, and letting people ask for the tool, is safer than posting the link.

**Do not:** write corporate-speak, post in groups that forbid self-promotion, or tag people who did not ask.

**Example:**
> Your offer letter says ₹12 lakh. Why does your bank only get ₹88,276 a month? 🤔
> Here's where it goes: ₹57,600 to your PF (and the same again from your employer), ₹23,088 of gratuity you receive when you leave after 5 years, and ₹2,400 of professional tax. Income tax is zero at this salary under the new regime.
> The surprising part: most of the "missing" money is yours, just later.
> Does your payslip look like this? Tell me what surprised you. (Link to the free calculator in the first comment.)

## X (Twitter)
**Voice: the RupeeCheck brand, sharp, dry-witted and number-first. Short sentences. One idea per post.** The platform rewards a strong opening line and a payoff. Be confident, not smug; never mock people who got a number wrong.

**Formats**
- **Single post:** hook, one or two numbers, the point. Under 280 characters. Image optional (`landscape`, 1600×900).
- **Thread:** 5 to 8 posts. Post 1 is the hook with the image. Each following post carries exactly one number or one step. The last post has the link and the assumptions. No "🧵👇" clichés unless it fits.
- **Reply:** during Budget week or a viral finance thread, reply with a verified calculation, not a pitch.

**Rules:** no more than one hashtag, usually none. The link goes in the last post or a reply, not the first post. Do not number every post "1/" unless the thread is long.

**Example thread opener and body:**
> ₹12 LPA is not ₹1 lakh a month. It's ₹88,276. Here's where ₹1.4 lakh a year goes 👇
> —
> Employer PF: ₹57,600. Never paid as cash, goes to your PF account.
> —
> Gratuity: ₹23,088. Paid when you leave after 5+ years.
> —
> Your own PF ₹57,600 + professional tax ₹2,400. Income tax: ₹0 under the new regime.
> —
> So ₹1,38,288 of the ₹1,40,688 is your own PF and gratuity. Your money, later. Free calculator (no signup, runs in your browser): <link>

## Reddit
**Voice: a real person who built something, speaking plainly. First person, humble, curious, zero marketing language.** Redditors detect and punish promotion. They reward people who answer the question properly and show their working.

**The pattern that works**
1. Answer the actual question first, in the first two lines.
2. Show the working: a small table or a short list of the numbers and the assumptions.
3. Mention caveats the way a careful person would ("this assumes the new regime and no other deductions").
4. Only at the end, and only if it helps or the rules allow, one sentence: "I made a free calculator that does this if you want to plug in your own numbers. I'm the maker, so take my bias into account." No tracking links, no shorteners.

**Rules to follow**
- Read the subreddit's rules (the sidebar). Many finance subreddits ban self-promotion outright, including communities such as r/IndiaInvestments, known for strict rules (check the current ones). If links are banned, answer without one and let the user's profile do the work.
- Use **one honest account** with the maker disclosed in the profile, not several accounts and not a fake persona.
- Participate more than you promote: for every post that mentions the site, the user should have helped in many threads without mentioning it.
- Never ask for upvotes, never reply to your own thread from another account, never delete and repost a removed post.

**Length:** aim for about 250 words and never much more than 300 (a table does not count against you, but keep it small). Long replies get skimmed or ignored; keep the answer, the table, the assumptions and one or two caveats, and cut the rest. State a firm verdict only as firmly as the assumptions allow ("by a fair margin on these assumptions", not "it isn't close").

**Tone check:** if a sentence could appear in an advertisement, rewrite it.

**Example (someone asks "new or old regime at ₹15 L?"):**
> For ₹15 L gross it comes down to how much you can actually claim. With only the standard deduction: new regime ≈ ₹97,500 tax, old regime ≈ ₹2.57 L. The old regime only catches up if your deductions on top of the standard one are above about ₹5.44 L.
> | Gross | Old regime wins only if deductions exceed |
> | ₹15 L | ₹5.44 L |
> | ₹20 L | ₹7.08 L |
> Typical deductions: 80C up to ₹1.5 L, NPS extra ₹50k, home-loan interest up to ₹2 L, HRA exemption, 80D. Add yours up. (Tax Year 2026-27, salaried, estimates.)
> I built a free calculator that compares both with your numbers; happy to share if useful. I'm the maker.

## Quora
**Voice: an authoritative but friendly explainer, someone who has done the maths.** Quora answers are long-lived and get found through search, so they should be complete and well structured.

**Pattern:** 150 to 350 words. Name any surprising or recent legal rule in the text (section or rule number, year), as on Reddit.
1. **First sentence = the direct answer** ("At ₹12 LPA you take home about ₹88,276 a month in the new regime.").
2. The working: a short list or table of the numbers and where they go.
3. The assumptions in one line, and the caveat that rules and payslips vary.
4. One closing line: "I built a free calculator for this on RupeeCheck (no signup)". Quora allows links, but a link without a real answer reads as spam. Disclose that you made it.

**Do not:** start with "Great question", pad with generic advice, or paste the same answer on many questions. Tailor each answer to the question asked.

## Choosing the format

| If the idea is… | Best on | Format |
|---|---|---|
| A myth broken by one number | Instagram, X | Myth-vs-math image; short post |
| A table the reader will save | Instagram, Facebook | Carousel or `compare` image |
| A step-by-step walkthrough | X, Instagram | Thread or carousel |
| A direct answer to someone's question | Reddit, Quora | Answer-first text with a small table |
| A relatable family situation | Facebook | Story plus one image |

## Language
English only for now. Use plain Indian English: "₹", lakh and crore, "CTC", "in-hand". Avoid American spellings of local terms and avoid slang that dates quickly.
