How is EMI calculated?

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ [(1 + r)n − 1], where P is the loan amount, r the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n the number of monthly instalments.

How to reduce your EMI burden

  • Make a larger down payment to lower the principal.
  • Negotiate or transfer your loan for a lower interest rate.
  • Prepay part of the principal whenever you get a bonus — early years are mostly interest.

Frequently asked questions

Is a longer tenure better?

A longer tenure lowers your EMI but increases the total interest paid. Choose the shortest tenure you can comfortably afford.

Does this include processing fees?

No. The calculator covers principal and interest only; processing fees and insurance are extra.

Is interest calculated on reducing balance?

Yes. Banks charge interest on the outstanding balance, which is what this calculator models.
