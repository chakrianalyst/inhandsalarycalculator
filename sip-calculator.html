How does a SIP work?

A Systematic Investment Plan invests a fixed amount in a mutual fund every month. Returns compound, so the longer you stay invested the faster your corpus grows. This calculator uses the effective monthly rate equivalent to your expected annual return and assumes investment at the start of each month.

Why step-up matters

Raising your SIP by even 10% each year as your salary grows can increase your final corpus dramatically compared with a flat SIP.

Frequently asked questions

Are SIP returns guaranteed?

No. Mutual fund returns are market-linked. The figures here are projections based on the return you enter, not a promise.

What return rate should I assume?

Equity funds have historically delivered roughly 10–14% over long periods, but be conservative in planning.

Is tax included?

No. Capital-gains tax on redemption is not deducted from the result.
