Monthly in-hand to LPA: what CTC do you need?

Most salary tools go from CTC to in-hand. When you are negotiating, you need the reverse: you know what you want in your bank each month, so what CTC should you ask for?

The short version
  • To take home ₹50,000 a month, you need a CTC of about {{LPA_50000}}.
  • ₹80,000 a month needs about {{LPA_80000}}, and ₹1 lakh a month about {{LPA_100000}}.
  • Your CTC is always well above 12 times your in-hand, because it includes PF, gratuity and income tax.

"80k per month in LPA": two different questions

People mean two things by this, with very different answers:

  • ₹80,000 in hand each month: you need a CTC of about {{LPA_80000}}.
  • ₹80,000 gross salary each month: that is ₹9.6 lakh a year of salary, before PF and tax.

Going the other way, a ₹4.5 LPA CTC is about {{LPA_45_INHAND}} a month in hand, and a ₹12 LPA CTC is about {{LPA_12_INHAND}}. See the in-hand for any CTC on our salary by CTC chart.

Using this in a negotiation

  • Work out the monthly amount you need first: rent, EMIs, savings and spending.
  • Find the CTC for it in the table, then ask about the offer’s structure. Variable pay, a capped PF or a higher basic all change the answer.
  • Treat variable pay and joining bonuses as extras. They are paid once, taxed at your top rate, and not always in full.

Frequently asked questions

What CTC gives ₹1 lakh a month in hand?

About {{LPA_100000}}, with a typical structure and the new tax regime. A fixed ₹1,800 PF instead of 12% PF lowers the CTC you need.

What is 80k per month in LPA?

If you mean ₹80,000 in hand, a CTC of about {{LPA_80000}}. If you mean ₹80,000 gross salary a month, that is ₹9.6 LPA before PF and tax.

Does variable pay count?

No. The table assumes the whole CTC is fixed pay. If part of your CTC is variable, your monthly in-hand is lower, and the variable comes later, after tax.

Why is the CTC so much more than 12 × in-hand?

CTC includes your employer’s PF and gratuity, which never reach your bank each month, plus your own PF and income tax, which are taken out of your salary.
