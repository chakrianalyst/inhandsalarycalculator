HRA exemption rules

If you live in rented accommodation and receive HRA, the least of the following is exempt from tax under section 10(13A): (1) actual HRA received, (2) rent paid minus 10% of basic salary, (3) 50% of basic for metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai) or 40% for others.

You must submit rent receipts to your employer, and landlord PAN is required if annual rent exceeds ₹1 lakh.

Frequently asked questions

Can I claim HRA in the new tax regime?

No. HRA exemption is available only under the old tax regime.

Can I claim HRA if I live with my parents?

Yes, if you actually pay them rent, they declare it as income, and you can prove payment.
